# Account settings

Use the account settings to manage your personal details and preferences.

## Update your profile

1. Open **Account settings**.
2. Select **Profile**.
3. Update your details.
4. Select **Save**.

## Change your password

1. Open **Account settings**.
2. Select **Security**.
3. Select **Change password**.
4. Enter your current password.
5. Enter and confirm your new password.
6. Select **Save**.
